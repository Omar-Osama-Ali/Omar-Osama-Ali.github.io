FAQ

Before we start.

What types of projects do you work on?

Websites, brand identities, social and digital content, product photography, presentations, and AI-assisted creative work.

Yes. I design and build responsive websites from scratch with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, as in NileSip Botanicals.

What information do you need to start?

What the project is, who it is for, any content you already have (text, logos, photos) and any deadline.

Can you help with school projects?

Yes, with design, presentations, websites and organizing material. I do not complete graded assignments for anyone.
